      How to read this graph

      CMU faculty connections use stable faculty IDs attached to the same deduplicated work in the publication source artifact. No topical or institutional relationship is inferred.

      Courtesy-only appointments are excluded by the source policy. Cross-appointed faculty receive automatic name labels after one of their departments is selected; hover, search, and direct selection remain available for everyone.

      Drag to pan, use the mouse wheel or zoom buttons to zoom, and select faculty nodes to compare their observed collaborators. Select a node again to remove it. Use faculty search for a keyboard-accessible route to every person.
